## 2. Problem Statement
Given two positive integers $a$ and $b$, compute their Greatest Common Divisor ($\gcd$) and Lowest Common Multiple ($	ext{lcm}$) using Euclid's algorithm in $\mathcal{O}(\log(\min(a, b)))$ time and $\mathcal{O}(1)$ auxiliary space.
