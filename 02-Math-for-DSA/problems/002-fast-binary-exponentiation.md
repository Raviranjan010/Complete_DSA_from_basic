## 2. Problem Statement
Compute $A^B \pmod M$ in $\mathcal{O}(\log B)$ time, where $B$ can be up to $10^{18}$.

---

## 3. Complexity Analysis
- **Time Complexity:** $\mathcal{O}(\log B)$
- **Space Complexity:** $\mathcal{O}(1)$ auxiliary memory
